vedafem

3D analysis and design of steel tank support structures, from the first sketch to the signed calculation report. Cross-checked against STAAD.Pro on four real Veda jobs: support reactions agree within 1.5%.

  • 3D frame analysis with P-Delta, modal and tension-only bracing
  • Wind to IS 875-3 and seismic to IS 1893-1 / -2, including tank sloshing
  • IS 800 member checks, connections, gussets, base plates and anchor bolts
  • Footings and pedestals to IS 456
  • Draw in the browser, generate a staging, or import a STAAD .std file
  • One-click calculation report as PDF
